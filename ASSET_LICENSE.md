Program code that the project is entitled to license is available under [GPL-3.0-only](LICENSE). Artwork, media, names, logos, and other brand assets may have separate rights and are not covered by the code license.

## Current status

Some included artwork contains real tobacco-brand packaging, and the project includes a lighter image atlas derived from third-party footage. Redistribution rights have not been confirmed for every asset. Do not assume that an asset may be repackaged or redistributed just because it is present in this repository.

The lighter atlas is derived from *Person Lighting up a Cigarette Lighter* by Monsieur Sylvain on [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/video/person-lighting-up-a-cigarette-lighter-5571499/). Review the applicable [Pexels license](https://www.pexels.com/license/) and terms before redistributing the atlas or a build that contains it.

## Contributed assets

Contributors must have the rights needed to submit and redistribute each asset. Include its creator or rights holder, source, license, attribution, and modifications. The license for each contributed asset must be confirmed before it is merged; no default license is implied.

Before publishing a build, source archive, screenshot, or video, review the rights for the specific assets it contains. Keep required notices and attribution with the relevant release.
